Our academic philosophy

We teach for the life beyond the classroom.

At Maria Theresa Ledochowska College, academic development is part of a wider formation. We want learners to acquire knowledge, develop sound judgement, discover their abilities and grow into responsible young people.

Our academic programme encourages curiosity, discipline, critical thinking and a commitment to doing one's best. These qualities are strengthened by the school's Catholic identity and its emphasis on moral and spiritual formation.
