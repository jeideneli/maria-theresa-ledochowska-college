Academic Excellence

Empowering ambitious young scholars through rigorous UNEB curriculums, world-class science laboratories, and digital innovation.

Academic Framework

Comprehensive Secondary Curriculum

Fully certified by the Ministry of Education & Sports and accredited by the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB).

Ordinary Level (UCE) — S.1 to S.4

Delivering the updated competence-based lower secondary curriculum. Students explore core sciences, humanities, business basics, computer studies, and practical vocational electives, emphasizing critical thinking, problem-solving, and project-based evaluation.

  • Continuous school-based assessment and UNEB national grading
  • Compulsory ICT and digital literacy foundation
  • Hands-on laboratory practicals starting from Senior One

Advanced Level (UACE) — S.5 & S.6

Specialized academic preparation for entry into top universities in Uganda (Makerere, Kyambogo, Mbarara, MUST) and overseas institutions. Students choose rigorous 3-principal combinations across Sciences, Arts, and Business tracks.

  • Science Combinations: BCM, PEM, PCB, MEG
  • Humanities Combinations: HEL, HED, DEG
  • Business Combinations: MEA, HEA, DEG
