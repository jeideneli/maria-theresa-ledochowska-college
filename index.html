Welcome to MTLC Lugazi

Quality & All-Round Education Spiced by Christian Values

Our Mission: To provide quality all-round education, spiced by Christian values, to produce responsible and God-fearing citizens. Maria Theresa Ledochowska College – Lugazi welcomes you.

We prepare ambitious scholars for national UNEB examinations (Center No. U2779) and global leadership. Through rigorous academics, dedicated tailoring and vocational skilling rooms, modern science and computer laboratories, spirited athletic programs, and vibrant spiritual formation, every learner discovers and maximizes their God-given potential.
