Faith & Formation

Spiritual Matters

Spiritual growth is an important part of the development of learners, teachers, and the wider school community. We nurture good morals, discipline, and responsibility through values of love, honesty, respect, kindness, forgiveness, peace, and unity.

Catechetical Formation

We identify learners who have not yet received catechetical instruction and help them access the religious formation they need in preparation for Holy Communion and other sacraments. The school guides learners and coordinates with the school chaplain so they can receive the required instruction.

Service at the Cathedral

Students are encouraged to serve as ushers at the Cathedral during Sunday Holy Mass and other religious celebrations. They welcome worshippers, guide them to their seats, maintain order, and help services run smoothly. Through this service, students practise humility, discipline, respect, hospitality, teamwork, and responsibility, while growing in commitment to God, the Church, and the Christian community.
