How we are guided

Responsible leadership for a thriving school.

The Board of Governors provides leadership, guidance, and oversight in the management and development of the institution. Working closely with the school administration, the Board helps the college operate effectively and achieve its educational goals.

Through its committees, the Board formulates policies, approves development plans and budgets, monitors school programmes, and safeguards the proper use of institutional resources. It also guides the college on discipline, staff welfare, infrastructure, and the continued improvement of teaching and learning.
