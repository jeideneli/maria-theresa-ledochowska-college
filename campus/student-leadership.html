Prefectorial Guild & student leadership
A voice for every learner

Helping our community learn, lead, and serve.

Student leadership at Maria Theresa Ledochowska College is an opportunity to practise responsibility in everyday school life. Prefects, student council members, and class representatives help learners communicate constructively and contribute to a respectful, orderly community.

Working with the school administration, student leaders support student welfare, encourage positive conduct, and help make campus activities inclusive and purposeful.

How student leadership works

Different roles. One shared purpose.

Each role gives students a constructive way to support their peers and contribute to the wider college community.

Prefectorial Guild

Prefects

Prefects model responsible conduct and help maintain a welcoming, orderly environment. They listen to fellow students, share concerns through appropriate school channels, and support the smooth running of daily activities.

  • Lead by example through respectful behaviour
  • Encourage care for shared spaces and school routines
  • Help communicate student concerns to staff
Student representation

Student Council

The Student Council provides a forum for student perspectives. Council representatives work with school leadership to raise issues affecting learners and contribute ideas for improving student welfare and campus activities.

  • Represent students in constructive discussion
  • Bring forward ideas and shared concerns
  • Support activities that strengthen school community
Everyday connection

Class Representatives

Class representatives help keep communication open between classmates, teachers, and student leaders. They encourage participation and help ensure class perspectives can be heard respectfully.

  • Share class feedback with the appropriate people
  • Encourage cooperation and participation
  • Promote respect and responsibility among peers
The MTLC approach

Leadership is a responsibility, not a title.

We encourage students to lead with humility, fairness, and care for others. Leadership is strengthened through listening, accountability, teamwork, and a willingness to serve the whole community.

Listen

Take student perspectives seriously and communicate with respect.

Serve

Put the wellbeing of the community ahead of personal recognition.

Act with integrity

Be fair, dependable, and accountable in carrying out responsibilities.

Work together

Collaborate with students, staff, and school administration.
